About ODLF

The Oratory & Debate League of Florida is envisioned as a student-centered community for public speaking, critical thinking, and competitive debate.

Our mission

To help students become confident communicators and careful thinkers through accessible, fair, and respectful speech and debate competition.

Our motto

“Speak with Purpose. Debate with Reason.”
